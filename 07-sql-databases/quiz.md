# Quiz

[Module home](README.md) | [Solutions](solutions.md)

Answer each question in your own words.

1. What property must every primary-key value have?
2. What does a foreign key protect?
3. Why is a comma-separated category list usually poor relational design?
4. How do `WHERE` and `HAVING` differ?
5. What is the difference between an inner join and a left join?
6. Why must null be checked with `IS NULL`?
7. Why can `COUNT(*)` and `COUNT(column)` return different results?
8. What problem do parameterized queries prevent, and what else do they handle?
9. Why can adding many indexes make writes slower?
10. What should a transaction do when its second required statement fails?
11. Name the four ACID properties.
12. Why should restoration be tested instead of assuming a backup is valid?

Strong answers describe behavior, not only expanded acronyms. Check the answer notes in [solutions](solutions.md) after completing every question.
